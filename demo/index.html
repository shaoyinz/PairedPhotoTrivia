Couples Photo Quiz

It finds the trips you two took together by comparing both of your photo libraries. Then it quizzes each of you on the other's photos: where was this, and when?

→You're playing as Maya. Her partner Theo approved today's five photos. Drop a pin, pick the date, see how close you got.

Code and product spec on GitHub

How it finds your trips

  1. Each phone reads when and where its photos were taken. That happens on the phone, and the photos stay there.
  2. It rounds every photo to a cell about 1 km wide and 1 hour long, then scrambles the cell with a key that only your two phones have.
  3. The phones compare scrambled cells. A cell you both have is an hour you were in the same place. Cells near home or work don't count.
  4. Shared hours close together become a trip once there are at least 5 photos between you. A trip runs overnight until one of you is back home.

What leaves your phone

Theo's photo at the McWay Falls overlook, Saturday at 1:10 pm, becomes one cell. Only the scrambled version syncs.

Stays on your phone

  • Your photos
  • Exact places and times
  • Where you live and work
  • Memory notes you keep private

Syncs between you two

  • Scrambled cells, which the server can't read
  • Photos the owner approves for a round
  • Scores and streaks

About this demo

Maya, Theo and their trips are made up. The photos show real places and come from Wikimedia Commons. The round follows the product spec: five photos from one trip, full points within 1 km of the spot and none past 500 km, then points for the right year, month and day. In the app, the owner previews each photo and can skip any of them before it's sent. The spec, plan and trip-detection code are on GitHub.
