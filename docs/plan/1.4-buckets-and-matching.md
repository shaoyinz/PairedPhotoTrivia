# 1.4 Buckets and matching (`buckets.py`, `matching.py`)

This is the part that mirrors what actually crosses the network, so keep the boundary honest:
`matching.py` may only ever see hashes, never coordinates or timestamps.

- [ ] `bucket_key(photo) -> (geohash6, hour_index)` where `hour_index = floor(utc_epoch / 3600)`
- [ ] `salted(key, salt) -> HMAC-SHA256(salt, f"{geohash6}|{hour_index}")`, truncated to 16 bytes
- [ ] `expand(key) -> set[key]`: the 8 geohash-6 neighbors + self, × hours {h-1, h, h+1}
- [ ] **Expand one side only.** Intersect `expand(A)` against raw `B`. Expanding both sides
      roughly triples the effective radius and window, and inflates false matches.
- [ ] `match(hashed_expanded_a, hashed_b) -> set[hash]`, then each side maps the surviving
      hashes back to its *own* local photos — exactly what a device can do alone
- [ ] Salt: 32 random bytes in `data/salt.key`, generated once, shared by both exports (it
      stands in for the salt the two paired devices will hold)
