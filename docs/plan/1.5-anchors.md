# 1.5 Anchors: home and work (`anchors.py`)

Needs *local* hours, which is why the export keeps `tz_offset_s` alongside UTC.

- [ ] Home = most frequent geohash-7 among photos with local hour in [00:00, 06:00)
- [ ] Work = most frequent geohash-7 among photos with local hour in [10:00, 16:00) on Mon–Fri
- [ ] Window: last 90 days of the export; if that is too sparse, widen and say so in the report
- [ ] Emit a `data/anchors_<person>.toml` the user edits by hand — this is the stand-in for the
      in-app confirm/edit screen, and hand-editing it is expected, not a fallback
