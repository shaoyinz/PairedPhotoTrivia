# 1.6 Away rule (`commute.py`)

**Status (2026-10-02):** done — `spike/tests/test_commute.py`, no real library needed. On the
synthetic fixture the inferred homes are 0.11 km apart, so shared. With the inferred anchors,
29 of A's 342 GPS photos and 16 of B's 324 are away, and those are exactly the photos on the
five planted days (Tahoe ×3, Monterey, the Half Moon Bay near-trip). Nights, weekends, both
commutes (B's is 43 km) and both workplaces all stay inside. Of §1.4's 61 matched keys, the 14
on planted days are away at their geohash-6 cell centers and the other 47 are not. Which point
stands for a bucket is §1.7's call. The near-trip is away, so only §1.7's ≥ 5 rule may drop
it. `photoquiz trips` now prints the rule it applies, then stops at §1.7 (`sessionize`).

- [x] `commute_buffer(home, work, km=5.0) -> shapely geometry`: buffer around the straight
      home→work segment, computed on the local km plane from `project.py` ([§1.1.0](1.1-scaffold.md) — not pyproj,
      not an azimuthal-equidistant projection; the flat local plane is accurate to 0.4% at 44 km
      and ports to Swift)
      *Returns `CommuteBuffer(origin, area)`, not a bare geometry: `area` is the shapely buffer,
      `origin` is the home its plane is centered on. A km-plane geometry cannot say which plane
      it is on, and one plane for the pair does not work, because a long-distance pair's homes
      can be a continent apart. `covers(p)` projects onto the buffer's own plane. `work = None`
      (or work on home) gives a disc. Shapely's round ends are inscribed polygons, at most 6 m
      inside the true circle at 5 km. A Swift port testing point-to-segment distance ≤ km agrees
      everywhere else.*
- [x] `is_away(point, buffers, shared_home: bool)`: shared home → outside **both**;
      different homes → outside **either** is enough (so visiting each other counts)
      *`is_away(p, buf_a, buf_b, *, shared_home)`, per the §1.1.5 contract. The edge counts as
      inside. It depends on `p` only through the two "inside" bits, so in phase 2 each phone can
      answer for its own buffer and neither needs the other's home or work.*
- [x] Decide `shared_home` by distance between the two homes (< 1 km ⇒ shared), overridable in
      the anchors file
      *`is_shared_home(a, b)`: strictly < 1 km on the flat plane (`project.distance_km`), never
      by geohash; the fixture's homes sit in neighboring cells. `resolve_shared_home(anchors_a,
      anchors_b)` applies the override: `shared_home = true|false` in either anchors file wins
      over distance, and the two files disagreeing is an error (`trips` exits 2). `anchors`
      writes it as a commented-out hint. The distance check needs both homes on one machine, which
      phase 2 cannot allow, so there the confirm screen asks "do you live together?" instead.
      This override stands in for that question.*
